About UgyenPee

Professional skills for Bhutan’s next generation.

Our institute is built around a simple idea: better skills create stronger professionals, stronger organisations and better opportunities for young people.

Our story

From training to real-world capability.

Professionalisation and effective management are increasingly important to Bhutan’s social and economic development. As technology, workplaces and industries evolve, young people need practical skills that connect education with real employment.

UgyenPee Professional Institute intends to support school and college leavers, job seekers and other learners by providing structured technical and professional training relevant to modern workplaces.

The institute aims to operate with qualified trainers, appropriate equipment, suitable learning environments and programmes aligned with applicable curricula and occupational standards.

◉

Vision

To become a leading centre for professional and management development, recognised for empowering people and practising respect for employees and society.

✦

Mission

To inspire, support and empower trainees through quality training that develops professional competence.

Purpose

To strengthen employability, professional confidence and Bhutan’s skilled human resources.
