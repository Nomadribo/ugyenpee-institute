Admissions

A clear path from interest to enrolment.

Applicants will be guided through a straightforward process, with final requirements depending on the programme selected.

How to apply

Four simple steps.

1

Choose your programme

Review course information and confirm that the programme matches your goals.

2

Submit your application

Complete the application form and provide the requested information.

3

Provide documents

Submit the required academic, identity and supporting documents.

4

Review & enrol

The institute reviews the application and shares the next steps with eligible applicants.

Documents to prepare

  • Academic transcripts / mark sheets
  • Citizenship Identity Card (CID) copy
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Security clearance, where applicable

Fee structure, scholarships and any government-funded slots will be displayed once confirmed.

Need help?

Ask before you apply.

Applicants should be able to understand eligibility, documents, fees and course outcomes before committing.
