About us

Nurturing skills for a brighter tomorrow.

UgyenPee Professional Institute aims to strengthen professional, technical and management skills among Bhutanese youth through practical, industry-relevant training.

Our goal is to help trainees build confidence, improve employability and prepare for meaningful careers in Bhutan and abroad, while contributing to the country’s long-term human resource development.

Our Vision

To become a leading institute for developing competent professionals and strengthening management capacity.

Our Mission

To inspire, support and empower trainees through quality training and relevant professional skills.

Career & job opportunities

Meaningful careers. Brighter futures.
